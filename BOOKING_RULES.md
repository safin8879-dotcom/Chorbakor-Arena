# CHORBAKOR ARENA — Proposed Booking Rules

- Operating window: 08:00 to 24:00 (Bangladesh time).
- Slot length: 120 minutes.
- Playing time proposal: 90 minutes, with 30 minutes for changeover/preparation.
- Standard example price: BDT 2,200 per slot (confirm before production).
- Advance example: BDT 500; remaining BDT 1,700 payable at the venue.
- Full payment: entire slot price paid online.
- Cancellation notice proposal: at least 24 hours before the slot; final refund terms must be approved by the owner and displayed before checkout.
- A booking is confirmed only after server-side payment verification or an authorized admin records an offline payment.
- A temporary hold must expire automatically if payment is not completed within the configured hold window.
- The system must prevent two active bookings for the same date and slot.

All amounts and policies above are draft settings. Confirm them with the business owner before launch.
